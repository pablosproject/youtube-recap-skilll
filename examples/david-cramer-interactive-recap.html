How To Ship Real Code With AI (Not Junk)

David Cramer, co-founder of Sentry, gives a grounded view of AI-assisted software development: AI is useful, but production-quality software still requires judgment, verification, accountability, and maintenance.

Watch on YouTube

Main Takeaway

AI can meaningfully improve developer workflows, especially for search, summarization, knowledge retrieval, and prototypes. But Cramer rejects “100x developer” hype: LLMs often generate bloated or risky code, and verification remains the hard part.

  • 01:12:00 His strongest opinion: AI is useful and here to stay, but today it still produces lots of bloated, overengineered, low-quality code.
  • 01:12:00 He calls AI “100% a bubble,” though bubbles do not necessarily pop immediately.

Conclusion

The message is not “AI replaces developers.” It is: developers need to learn where AI helps, where it creates risk, and how to keep engineering standards intact while using it.
